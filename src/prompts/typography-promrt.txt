============================================================
TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM — THE MERIDIAN
============================================================

FONT FAMILIES (never change):
- Serif = Fraunces → used for headlines, pull quotes, drop caps
- Sans = Plus Jakarta Sans → used for body, UI, tags, meta
- Mono = Geist Mono → used for code only

IMPORTANT: h1, h2, h3, h4 automatically use Fraunces (set globally).

WRITING STYLE — THE MERIDIAN VOICE
============================================================

TONE:
- Calm, intelligent, warm, editorial.
- Confident but not loud. Thoughtful, not rushed.
- No hype. No clickbait. No emojis in body copy.
- Feels like a letter from a well-read friend.

SENTENCE RHYTHM:
- Mix short and long sentences.
- One idea per paragraph. Break early and often.
- Active voice preferred.

LENGTH:
- Lead paragraph: 1–2 sentences.
- Body paragraphs: 2–4 sentences.
- Whole article: match requested read time (roughly 200 words per minute).

STRUCTURE:
- Open with a hook, not a summary.
- Use subheadings every 3–5 paragraphs.
- End with a resonant closing line, not a recap.

FORBIDDEN:
- No "In today's fast-paced world..."
- No "Let's dive in..."
- No "In conclusion..."
- No emojis.
- No exclamation marks in body copy.
- No bullet spam — use lists only when truly needed.
